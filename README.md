## ✨ Features

- 🎼 **Dastgah Selection:** Explore different Iranian musical Dastgahs.
- 🎹 **Scale Calculator:** Calculate scale degrees and intervals.
- 🎵 **Root Note Selection:** Choose your desired tonic.
- ♯ **Accidental Selection:** Choose between available accidental options.
- 🎶 **Musical Notation:** View notes and their corresponding musical labels.
- 🌍 **Bilingual Interface:** Switch between Persian and English.
- 🌙 **Dark & Light Modes:** Choose your preferred appearance.
- ⭐ **Favorites:** Save your favorite musical scales.
- ⏱️ **Metronome:** Access a built-in metronome.
- 📋 **Easy Copy:** Copy displayed musical information.

---

## 🎼 Supported Dastgahs

Koronosori includes a selection of traditional Iranian Dastgahs:

- Shur
- Segah
- Chahargah
- Mahur
- Homayoun
- Nava
- Rast-Panjgah

## 🎯 Purpose

Koronosori aims to make exploring Iranian musical structures easier and more accessible through an intuitive interface.

Whether you are a music student, musician, or simply interested in Persian traditional music, Koronosori offers a convenient way to explore musical scales and their structures.
